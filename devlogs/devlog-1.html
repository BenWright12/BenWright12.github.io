Why My Facilities Experience Fuels My Healthcare Vision

How orchestrating daily operations at Google sites scales directly into engineering secure, fault tolerant digital care architectures.

On the surface, pivoting from hospitality management and large scale site operations into Data Science and Artificial Intelligence might seem like a sudden leap. However, as I begin my MSc journey at Birkbeck and lay the groundwork for The Healthcare Companion (Evie), I am finding that my operational background is not just relevant but it is my greatest technical asset.

Operations is a Data Integrity Problem

For the past several years, I have orchestrated daily operational conditions across high stakes environments, culminating in my current role overseeing multiple Google office sites in London with CBRE. In these roles, success isn't just about managing people, it is fundamentally about managing fragmented, cross departmental datasets.

Whether I was analysing CRM telemetry to track venue conversion at Whistle Punks or maintaining strict administrative spreadsheets for Google stakeholders, the core directive was always the same: Data must be clean, actionable, and absolutely secure.

"In site operations, a broken administrative pipeline results in disrupted workflows for hundreds of people. In healthcare AI, a compromised data pipeline risks patient privacy. The foundational logic required to prevent both is identical."

Translating Audits into Database Architecture

A large portion of my professional experience revolves around strict financial auditing, data integrity, and compliance. Moving into systems engineering, this exact mindset governs how I approach database design.

As I dive into the database design modules at Birkbeck, I am immediately applying these lessons to the conceptualisation of The Healthcare Companion. We are not just building an AI that observes, we are building a system that must actively protect its user. This means our database specifications require SQLite structures locked down with SQLCipher encryption at the edge level, ensuring that even if physical hardware is compromised, the telemetry cannot be audited by bad actors.

Stakeholder Management to System Engineering

Leading high stakes operations means serving as the primary point of contact for stakeholders, translating multi-team delivery constraints into clear, strategic communications. In software engineering, this is known as systems integration.

Designing autonomous AI agents and computer vision pipelines requires taking distinct, isolated departments (the camera feed, the pose estimation model, the local database) and forcing them to communicate seamlessly without dropping performance.

Looking Forward

I am excited to continue documenting this journey. While the theoretical math and machine learning models I am studying at Birkbeck are the engine of my upcoming projects, my operational background serves as the skeleton which will ensure the system is secure, scalable, and resilient enough for the real world.
